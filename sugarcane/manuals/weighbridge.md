# Weighman and yard supervisor

The guide **Gate, gross & tare** (PDF, with screenshots) and its video are at the top of this page.

The screen shows the **live weight** from the indicator (green = stable), the **platform** beams, and the bridge's **cameras**.
The **grower display** facing the drivers shows the same weight, the vehicle, the stage and the result, in English and Urdu.

## Gross weighman — Cane Procurement → Gross · 1st weight
1. **Scan the token** (or the indent, or type the vehicle no.). Or click the vehicle in *Waiting for gross*.
2. Watch the **platform** box. If a beam is **cut**, the vehicle is not fully on the deck: the **red light** comes on and the weight is refused. Ask the driver to move forward or back.
3. When every beam is clear and the weight is stable, the screen **counts down and takes the weight by itself** (auto-weigh). Or click **Take Gross weight**. **Stop auto** pauses it.
4. The weight and a photo from each camera are saved, and the **gross slip** prints. The truck goes to the yard to unload.

## Tare weighman — Cane Procurement → Tare · 2nd weight
1. **Scan the gross slip** (or the token, the indent, the vehicle no.). Or click the vehicle in *Waiting for tare*.
2. **Same vehicle?** Each photo taken at gross is shown beside the same camera live now. Body, trolley, wheels and plate must match; only the load is gone. **Not the same: don't tick, and call the Yard Supervisor.**
3. Tick **Same vehicle**. With the platform clear and the empty weight stable, the tare is taken (auto or by the button).
4. The **CPR** and the **TPT bilty** are made and print at once. The grower gets an SMS, and the grower display shows the **net weight**.

A bridge that takes both weights can use **Weighbridge (both)**: it takes whichever weight is due on the scanned token.

## The system refuses when
- the vehicle is not fully on the platform (a beam is cut)
- the reading is not stable, or is old (is the weighbridge agent running?)
- the weight is under the lowest accepted, or outside the vehicle type's gross / tare limits
- the token is at the other stage. The screen links you to the right one.
- at tare, *Same vehicle* is not ticked while gross photos exist.

Only a **Cane Admin** may type a weight, for example when the indicator is offline, and must write why. It is flagged on the CPR and the slip.

## Yard supervisor
**Yard board**: trucks waiting for gross, and trucks waiting for tare (with their gross).

- Any truck over 12 hours inside is flagged. Find out why: no unloading crew, a breakdown, a dispute.
- Lanes show where the trucks are queued.
- A tare weighman calls you when a vehicle doesn't match its gross photos.
