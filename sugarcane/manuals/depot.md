# Depot user

You see only your own depot.

## Buying cane at the depot
- **Depots → Buy cane at a depot.** It works like the mill gate: scan the indent, issue the token, weigh gross and tare on the depot's weighbridge.
- The depot CPR is made at the depot's rates.

## Sending cane to the mill
1. **Dispatch trucks.** When the depot holds a truckload (the screen shows the balance), create a dispatch: truck, contractor and driver.
2. Weigh the **empty truck (depot tare)**, load it, and weigh it **loaded (depot gross)**. The truck leaves.
3. At the mill it is weighed again (mill gross, mill tare). The **transit shortage** is worked out, with the freight and any recovery from the contractor.

## Watch
The **Depot dashboard** shows cane held, trucks on the road, and the shortage by day.
Report: *Depot Excess Short*.
