# Cane cashier — the TPT cash counter

The guide **TPT cash counter** (PDF, with screenshots) and its video are at the top of this page.

The till always shows: **opening + received from strong room − returned to strong room − cash paid = current balance**.

## Start the shift
1. **Cane Procurement → TPT cash counter.** Choose the **shift**: A 07–15, B 15–23 or C 23–07.
2. **Open this counter** on **Cash Counter 1** or **2**. The opening balance is that counter's last counted close.
3. **Receive from strong room**: the amount and the strong room voucher no. or who brought it.
4. On the screen facing the drivers, open **Payee displays → your counter** (full screen: F11).

## Pay bilties
1. The driver gives his **bilty** and **CNIC**. **Scan the bilty**: it goes into the basket. Scan more to pay several together.
2. **Pay**. Check *Paid to* and the **CNIC** against the card. If the bilties belong to different drivers, pay one person (e.g. the transporter) and write who.
3. Choose:
   - **Cash** from the till. You can't pay more than the till holds.
   - **JazzCash**: send the amount from the mill's JazzCash account, then enter the wallet no. and the **transaction ID (TID)**.
4. The bilty slips print marked *Paid*, and the payee display shows **PAID**.

## Your payments
**This shift** and **My payments** (with dates) list everything you paid, with cash and JazzCash totals. Use **list** or **card** view.

## End the shift
1. Return extra cash with **Return to strong room**.
2. **Close counter**: count the notes (Rs 5,000 … Rs 10), with coins as an amount.
3. A **short** or **excess** needs a reason. It is posted to *Cash Short and Excess*.
4. The **Z report** (A4) prints. Sign it with the strong room in-charge. **X report** prints the shift so far at any time.

## Mistakes
A payment made by mistake is **reversed with a reason** by a Cane Admin. The bilty goes back to unpaid, and the cash back into the till.
