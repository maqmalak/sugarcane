# ELP — loading points and gate verification

## LP operator (ELP app on the phone)
1. Sign in to the **ELP** app (a Cane Manager approves a new phone).
2. When a truck is loaded on an indent, choose **Register load**:
   - scan or type the indent
   - enter the vehicle number and the driver's name and CNIC
3. Take the **four photos** the app asks for: number plate, indent, driver, body left. A blurred or dark photo is refused.
4. **Save** inside the loading point's geofence. The app shows whether your GPS is inside it.
5. The truck leaves. The registration travels with it to the mill gate. It is sent at once, or from the outbox when there is network.

## ELP auditor (web app)
1. **ELP → Gate & verification**: trucks that reached the gate, with the loading point photos beside the gate photos.
2. Compare them: the same truck and plate, the same indent, a load that looks the same, a journey time close to the standard.
3. **Accept** the load, or **Reject** it with a reason.
   - An accepted load gets an *ELP-verified* token, and the transport (TPT) is paid.
   - A rejected load comes in at the gate rates.
4. Watch **On the way** (registered, not arrived) and **Overdue** (not at the gate long after its journey time).

## Reports
*ELP Pending Arrival*, *ELP Pending Verification*, *ELP Journey Time*, *ELP LP Wise Summary*, *ELP Vehicles Not Arrived*.
