# Surveyor — the EagleEye app

## First day
1. Install **EagleEye** from the link the IT office gives you.
2. Sign in with your ERP email and password. A new phone waits until a **Cane Manager approves it**; you'll be told when.
3. Set a 4–8 digit **PIN**. The app asks for it after 2 minutes in the background. 5 wrong PINs sign you out.

## Each day
1. **Check in**: take a selfie inside your circle. The app records your GPS route while you're on duty.
2. Open **Survey → New**. Choose the village and the grower:
   - a passbook grower: search by name, CNIC or passbook number
   - a new grower: choose *Non-Passbook Grower* and enter the name, father's name and CNIC
3. **Field boundary.** Walk to each corner and press **Add corner from GPS** (it averages 6 seconds), or tap the corners on the satellite map. The app shows the area in acres and kanal.
4. Enter the crop details:
   - variety; plant or ratoon (and the ratoon number)
   - sowing date
   - condition; disease and its %
   - the land: total, cane, empty
5. Take the **photos**: the field, and the grower if asked. A blurred or dark photo is refused, so take it again.
6. **Save.** It goes to the outbox and is sent when there is a network, every 15 minutes or at once with the cloud button.
7. **Check out** at the end of the day.

## Rules
- The field must be inside its village. An area very different from what the grower says is flagged for the circle officer.
- A field that overlaps another field goes to the circle officer for review. No indent is made for it until that's cleared.
- Nothing is lost offline: every survey has its own number, so a re-send never makes a duplicate.

## Problems
- **"Device not approved"**: ask your Cane Manager to approve the phone.
- **GPS accuracy too low**: stand in the open and wait a few seconds.
- **Outbox not emptying**: check the network, then press the cloud button. If it still fails, tell the IT office the error shown.
