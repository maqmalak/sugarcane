# Weighman and yard supervisor

## Gross weighman
1. **Cane Procurement → Weighbridge.** The live indicator reading is at the top: green = stable.
2. When the truck is fully on the bridge and the reading is stable, **scan the token**.
3. **Take gross.** The weight saved is the indicator's own stable reading, less than 10 seconds old. You can't type it.
4. The truck goes to the yard to unload.

## Tare weighman
1. Scan the token of the empty truck and **Take tare**.
2. The **CPR** is made at once and its slip prints: net, binding, payable maund, value, deductions, payable.
   - The grower gets an SMS. A transport bilty is made for the driver.

## The system refuses when
- the reading is not stable, or is old (is the indicator agent running?)
- the weight is under the lowest accepted, or outside the vehicle type's gross / tare limits
- the token is for another weighment step.

Only a **Cane Admin** may type a weight, for example when the indicator is offline, and must write why. It is flagged on the CPR.

## Yard supervisor
**Yard board**: trucks waiting for gross, and trucks waiting for tare (with their gross).

- Any truck over 12 hours inside is flagged. Find out why: no unloading crew, a breakdown, a dispute.
- Lanes show where the trucks are queued.
