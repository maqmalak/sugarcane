# Cane cashier — the TPT cash counter

1. **Cane Procurement → TPT cash counter.** Unpaid bilties are listed. Search by the bilty or CPR number, the vehicle, or the driver.
2. Check the **driver's CNIC** against the bilty. If someone else collects for the driver, enter their name and CNIC.
3. **Pay.** The payment is posted (cash counter → TPT payable) and the bilty slip prints with *Paid*.
4. At the end of the shift, count the cash against the counter's total for the day.

## Mistakes
A payment made by mistake is **reversed with a reason** (Cane Accountant). The bilty goes back to unpaid.
