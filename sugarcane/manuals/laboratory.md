# Laboratory and production

## Lab chemist — through the crushing day
1. **Laboratory → Hourly samples**: the grid of sample types × hours.
   - Enter Pol, Brix, moisture and so on as each sample is tested. Purity is worked out.
   - A value outside its limits is shown in red. Check it before saving.
2. **Vehicle samples**: scan the CPR of a core-sampled truck and enter Brix, Pol and fibre. CCS and recovery are worked out per variety.
3. **Mill stoppages**: record each stoppage with its start, end (when it ends), category, section and reason.
   - The MIS wall shows a red banner while one is open.

## Lab chemist — after the crushing day (07:00)
1. **Daily manufacturing report** for the day. Cane received and the yard come from the CPRs.
2. Enter:
   - the yard closing
   - the sugar bags, molasses, bagasse and press mud
3. **Prepare** it: the recovery, losses and averages are worked out from the samples.

## Production manager
1. Open the prepared DMR, check it, and **Approve**.
2. Approval posts the production: the cane crushed out of the yard, the sugar bags into the godown, and the by-products at their value.
3. **Production → Plant levels**: the latest readings with their normal ranges. Out-of-range points show on the MIS wall.

## Reports
*Variety Wise Recovery*, *Daily Manufacturing Summary*, *Cost of Production per Bag*.
