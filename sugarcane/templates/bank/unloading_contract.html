1 · Work

The contractor unloads all cane brought to the mill yard{% if doc.include_depot_trucks %}, including trucks from the mill's depots{% endif %}, with his own labour, and keeps the carrier and feeding area clear so that crushing does not stop for want of cane.

4 · Billing and payment

The mill makes the contractor bill for each period from the CPRs, depot dispatches and the stoppage log. Deductions are shown on the bill. The net amount is paid to the contractor's account after the bill is posted.
