# HR officer — attendance and payroll

## People
- Each **Employee** has:
  - department, designation and employment type (*Seasonal* for crushing-season staff)
  - default shift (A 06–14, B 14–22, C 22–06, General 09–17)
  - **Attendance Device ID** (the user number on the biometric clock)
  - CNIC, EOBI and PESSI numbers
- A **Salary Structure Assignment** gives the employee *Mill Staff – Monthly* with their base pay and the income tax slab.

## Attendance
- The biometric clocks send every punch to the ERP. HRMS marks attendance from the shift: present, half day, absent, **late** after a 10-minute grace.
- **HR & Payroll page**: present, absent and late today; attendance for 14 days; staff by department.
- **Leave**: Leave Applications (casual 10, sick 8, annual 14, CPL, leave without pay) approved by the leave approver.

## Month end
1. **Apply late penalties** on the HR page: every 3 late marks in the month = one day's pay, as an Additional Salary. It is made once a month.
2. **Payroll Entry** for the month: salary slips with EOBI, PF, group insurance and income tax (Finance Act 2025 slabs), then submit.
3. Overtime, bonus or arrears: **Additional Salary**.
4. Leaving staff: **Full and Final Statement** (gratuity at 30 days' basic per year of service).

## Reports
*Late Coming Register*, *Monthly Attendance Sheet*, *Salary Register*, *Income Tax Deductions*.
