# Circle officer

You supervise the surveyors and the growers of your circles. You see only your own circles.

## Daily
1. **EagleEye Survey → Approvals inbox**: survey overlaps waiting for you. For each:
   - open the map
   - decide which field is right (or both, with the overlap noted)
   - *Accept* or *Reject*, with a remark
2. **Field Force → Insights**: who is surveying, check-ins outside the geofence, each surveyor against their target.
3. **Field Force → Users & devices**: approve or block a surveyor's phone.

## Weekly
- **Survey Map**: filter by village or variety. Look for fields not yet surveyed and for gaps between neighbouring fields.
- **Season Quotas**: your circle's cane against its quota.
- **Growers**: correct a grower's mobile number (it is used for SMS), bank account and passbook.

## Loans
- **Cane Accounts → Grower loans**: enter a grower's loan application. The eligibility box shows what the grower can borrow and why. The Cane Manager approves it.
- **Grower statement**: a grower's CPRs, payments and loan balance. Use it to answer growers' questions.

## Reports
*Circle Wise Survey Summary*, *Grower Wise Survey Summary*, *Surveyor Attendance Report*, *Circle Wise Performance* (MIS).

## Grower loans
You can enter a grower's **loan request** (Cane Accounts → Grower loans), and do or check the **loan field survey** (Loan field surveys): acres, crop condition, photos, GPS and a recommendation. The guide **Grower loans** and its video are at the top of this page.
