# Cane manager, cane admin and management

## Approvals (Desktop → Approvals inbox)
- **Indent batches**: authorise, or reject with a reason.
- **Grower loans**: approve after the **field survey**, and not above the surveyor's recommended amount. You may lower it, never raise it. Or **reject** with a reason. A loan the surveyor did not recommend can't be approved.
- **Survey devices**: approve a new surveyor's or LP operator's phone; block a lost one.
- **Survey overlaps** that a circle officer escalates.

## Overrides (Cane Admin only, always with a note)
- **Type a weight** when an indicator is offline.
- Accept a weight **outside the vehicle limits**.
- Cancel a CPR that is not yet paid, its cess not deposited.
- Write off a loan.

Every override is recorded with your name and the note. They are reviewed in the reports.

## The MIS wall (MIS → Mill right now)
- **What it shows:**
  - cane bought today by hour; trucks in the yard and on the road
  - unpaid to growers; the last DMR and stock
  - plant points out of range; stoppages, with a red banner while the mill is stopped
  - the season against target; circles against quota
- **Wall mode** hides the menus for a TV. It refreshes itself.

## Grower messages (MIS → Grower messages)
- **Log**: every SMS / WhatsApp sent and received, with its status.
- **Templates**: change the wording per event and language (English, Roman Urdu, Urdu). The preview shows the length and SMS parts.
- **Settings**: gateway, sender ID, quiet hours, the daily limit, and a test number. While a test number is set, every message goes to it.

## Weighbridges and counters
- **Platform sensors**: each weighbridge's beams show on its screen. A vehicle not fully on the deck can't be weighed, and the red light comes on. Switch sensors and **auto-weigh** on per terminal.
- **Cameras**: gate and weighbridge cameras photograph every vehicle. At tare, the weighman confirms it is the same vehicle as at gross.
- **Cash counters**: each cashier opens a counter for a shift. The **Daily cashier summary** and its **Excess & short** tab show every shortage, with its reason.
- **Displays**: *Payee displays* — the grower display at each weighbridge, the payee display at each counter.

## Settings and masters (Setup)
- **Seasons:** rates, quotas, the bag weight.
- **Areas:** circles, villages, sources, loading points, depots.
- **Vehicles:** vehicle types and their weight limits.
- **Gate:** weighbridge terminals (IP, platform sensors, auto-weigh), CCTV cameras, drivers.
- **Counters:** cash counters and their cash accounts. Sugar Settings holds the strong room, short / excess and JazzCash accounts.
- **Loans:** loan types: limits, minimum surveyed acres, field survey before approval.
- **Sugar Settings:** accounts, bank letters, SMS, tolerances.

Change rates only at a season boundary, or with the accountant. Tokens copy the rates at issue.
