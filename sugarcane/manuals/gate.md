# Token clerk — the mill gate

The guide **Gate, gross & tare** (PDF, with screenshots) and its video are at the top of this page.

1. **Cane Procurement → Gate · token issue.** The **mill gate cameras** play live above the form: number plate, cabin, left and right.
2. **Scan the indent** barcode, or type the number, or choose the truck's **ELP registration**.
3. The screen shows:
   - the grower, the indent's validity, the entry type (*ELP verified* or *Gate*)
   - the rates for this load: cane price, cess, subsidies, TPT
   - anything that blocks it: an expired or used indent, a blacklisted vehicle or driver, a truck already inside
4. Enter or confirm the **vehicle number** and **vehicle type**.
5. **Driver.** Use either:
   - the **driver code** (e.g. `DRV-00001`, or just `1`) and press **Enter**: name, CNIC and mobile fill in; or
   - the driver's **name and CNIC**. A CNIC already on file picks that driver. A new CNIC registers a new driver with a code when the token is issued.
6. **Issue token & print.** Each gate camera saves a photo of the vehicle with the token. The truck goes to **Gross · 1st weight**.

## Rules
- One open token per vehicle.
- An indent can be used once, and only on its valid days.
- Driver CNIC: 13 digits (`33100-8680138-5`). Mobile: 11 digits starting 03 (`03007212058`). Both are checked as you type.
- Without ELP verification, transport is paid at the gate rate (often none). The screen says so before you issue.

## Mistakes
A token issued by mistake can be **cancelled with a reason** before the gross weight. After that, ask the Cane Admin.
