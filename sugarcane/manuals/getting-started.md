# Getting started

## Signing in
1. Open the ERP address your IT office gave you, then **/sugar**: for example `https://erp…/sugar`.
2. Sign in with your email and password. Never share your account: every weight, payment and approval is recorded against the person who made it.
3. Forgot your password? Ask the IT office to reset it. After 5 wrong tries the account is locked for a while.

## The Desktop
- After signing in you see the **Desktop**: the apps of your role (Indenting, Cane Procurement, Laboratory…), with alerts and anything waiting for your approval.
- Click an app to open it. Its menu is on the left. On a phone, the ☰ button shows the menu.

## Moving around
- The **Back** button at the top of every page returns you to the page you came from, with your filters as you left them.
- **Search** (Ctrl + K) finds a grower, CPR, indent, token or any record by its number or name.
- The bell shows notifications, such as an approval you are waiting for or an overlap to review.

## Reports
- **Report Centre** lists every report your role may open.
- Pick a report, set the dates and filters, and use **Excel** or **PDF** to download it. PDFs print on the company letterhead.

## When something is refused
The ERP checks the mill's rules and says why it refuses. For example: "the weight is not stable yet", "this indent expired on 4 Nov", or "a paid CPR can't be cancelled". Read the message: it tells you what to do. If you think a rule is wrong, tell your manager. Don't work around it.

## Help
- **Help** in your apps menu shows the manual for each of your roles.
- **Help → Guides & videos** has the step-by-step A4 guides (PDF, with screenshots) and the demo videos. They are listed in the order the work flows:
  1. Process flow & DFD
  2. Setup
  3. EagleEye e-survey
  4. Indenting and the ELP
  5. Gate, gross & tare
  6. Depot purchase
  7. TPT cash counter
  8. Grower payments
  9. Grower loans
  10. Laboratory & production
  11. Costing
  12. Printed documents
- Guides for your own roles are marked *yours*. The manual pages also show their guide and video at the top.
