# Indent clerk

Indents call the growers' cane to the mill as the mill can crush it.

## Generating indents (usually weekly)
1. **Indenting → Generate indents.** Choose the dates and, if needed, a circle.
2. The **preview** shows:
   - each circle's entitlement (surveyed area × yield × quota rule)
   - the indents per day against the mill's daily intake
   - any warnings: a circle over its quota, fields held for an overlap review
3. **Generate.** The indents are made in one *Indent Batch* per circle, as drafts.
4. The **Cane Manager authorises** each batch from the *Approvals inbox* (or rejects it; its indents are deleted).
   - On authorisation each grower gets an SMS with the indent number, the load and the days it is valid.
5. **Print** the authorised indents: one slip each, with a barcode. Circle staff hand them out.

## Rules
- Running the generator twice for the same dates issues nothing new.
- An indent is valid only for its days. The day after, it **expires**, and the grower is reminded by SMS the day before.
- A used indent shows its gate token. It can't be used again.

## Looking up an indent
**Look up an indent**: scan the barcode or type the number. It shows the grower, validity, status and, once used, the token and CPR.

## Reports
*Indent Register*, *Daily Indent Schedule*, *Circle Wise Indent Summary*, *Grower Indent Balance*.
