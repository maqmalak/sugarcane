# Cane accountant

## Every morning
1. **Cane Procurement → Day close.** Check yesterday's crushing day:
   - every CPR posted
   - the purchase invoice made
   - the five checks green: clearing 0; TPT, cess, grower payable and loans tied
2. **Close the day.** The scheduler closes it at 07:15 if you haven't. A day with a problem stays open and is listed on the Desktop.

## Paying growers
1. **Cane Accounts → Payment scroll.** Start from the unpaid CPRs up to a date, or empty.
2. **Scan CPR barcodes**, or type a grower ID or CNIC to add all their unpaid CPRs. Duplicates are dropped.
3. Check the scroll: one payment per grower. Growers without an IBAN go on a cheque scroll.
4. **Pay.** Payment Entries are posted per grower, the CPRs are marked paid, and each grower gets an SMS.
5. Download the **bank file** and print the **payment schedule** and the **instruction letter for each bank**. Every print is kept in the document vault.
6. Bank returns a payment? Open the scroll line and choose **stop payment**: the letter prints, and the request is recorded.

## Monthly
- **Road cess**: deposit last month's cess with the challan number.
- **Grower loans → Accrue markup** for the month (the scheduler does it on the 1st).
- **Contractor bills**: unloading (with idle payment for stoppages), loading and depot statements. Post them.

## Loans
Enter or check applications. The Cane Manager approves; you **disburse**. Recovery from each CPR is automatic.

## Reports
*Grower Ledger*, *Grower Payable Aging*, *Grower Trial Balance*, *CPR Paid Unpaid*, *Road Cess Deducted vs Deposited*, *Grower Loan Register*, *Cane Cost per Maund*.
