# Cost accountant — cost of production

The mill uses process costing with by-product credit:

**cane cost + processing cost − by-product credit (NRV) = net cost of sugar**, then per kg, per 50 kg bag and per ton of cane.

## Before the season
1. **By-product Rates** (Production → Costing): for each season and by-product, enter:
   - the sale rate per ton
   - the selling and handling cost per ton
   - the date it is valid from

   The NRV per kg is worked out, and production uses the rate valid on its day.
2. **Cost Allocation Rules**: how each service department (Workshop, Stores, Administration) is shared out to the production departments: fixed %, equal, or by hours.
3. **Crushing Season → Budgeted processing cost per ton of cane**: it gives the daily provisional cost.
4. **Sugar Settings → Production Costing:**
   - credit bagasse burnt in own boilers (on or off)
   - the Off-season cost centre
   - profit margin %
   - GST %

## Every day (automatic)
- When the production manager approves a DMR, production posts one Manufacture entry per sugar grade.
  - Cane is consumed at its actual weight.
  - Bags go into the godown.
  - By-products are credited at NRV.
  - Burnt bagasse is issued to the Boiler House as Power & Steam.
- Each production day shows a **provisional cost per bag**: stock cost + the mill's own cane costs + the budgeted processing per ton.

## Month end — the Costing Run
1. Make sure every expense of the month is booked to its **cost centre**:
   - salaries by department (payroll)
   - power bills, repairs, depreciation, insurance
   - store issues to departments
   - off-season costs on the Off-season cost centre
2. **New Costing Run**: the month's dates, run type *Monthly*. Saving shows:
   - **Prime cost:** direct materials (cane, chemicals, packing) + direct labour
   - **Factory overhead (FOH):** power and fuel, stores, repairs, depreciation, overheads, service departments allocated in, the off-season share
   - **Cost of production** = prime cost + FOH
   - **By-product credit** and the **net cost**
   - **Process cost:** each process (milling, clarification and boiling, centrifugal and packing, steam and power, laboratory) with its cost per ton of cane and per bag
   - **Per 50 kg bag:** cost before tax → + profit margin → ex-mill price before GST → + GST → price after GST; also the cost with GST (break-even)
   - **Final − provisional per bag**
3. Check it, then **Submit**. With *Carry the full cost into sugar stock* ticked, the sugar in the godown on the last day is revalued to the full cost per bag. Sales after that day are reposted at the new rate.

## Season close
The last run of the season is a **Season Close**:
- It ends on the season's last day.
- It takes the rest of the off-season pool.
- It moves each by-product's **variance** (actual sale − selling cost − NRV credited) to the *By-product Variance* account.
